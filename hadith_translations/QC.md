## Cleaning

- Whitespace trimmed/collapsed, literal `\n` turned into line breaks (Russian), soft hyphens removed.
- Bare footnote markers like `[1]` / `[১]` removed (the footnote bodies are not in the dataset).
- Bengali: leading `।` removed; Bukhari/Malik entries that start with the chapter heading (Arabic + Bengali) and the hadith number have the heading removed up to the number.
- Turkish (all books carry the print edition apparatus): the appended commentary (Bukhari: Fath al-Bari) and cross-references are cut at the first marker (`Fethu'l-Bari Açıklaması`, `Tekrar(ı):`, `İZAHI İÇİN BURAYA TIKLA`, `Diğer tahric`, `Bu Hadis Sadece Muvatta’da var`, `İzah`, `Tuhfetu’l-Eşraf`, `Hadisin geçtiği diğer yerler`, `Bu Hadis N numara…`, `Hadisten/Bu Hadislerden Çıkarılan Sonuçlar`, `Açıklaması:`, an Arabic `باب` next-chapter heading, and ALL-CAPS editorial notes such as `AÇIKLAMASI 380. sayfa’da`); anything still > 2× the median length, or < ½ the length of the English translation of the same hadith (partial translations; not applied to Malik, whose English keeps the full isnad), is dropped.
- Arabic is never modified; no text is generated.
